01 About the company

Reliable supply. Clear communication. International cooperation.

A systematic approach to international trade

Trustact Ltd is a private limited company incorporated in England and Wales on 18 February 2023. The company coordinates product sourcing, supplier selection and ordering between the Chinese market and international clients.

  • Researching supply options for a wide range of industrial and commercial products
  • Business communication with manufacturers and suppliers
  • Coordination of pricing, specifications and order details

02 Services

From the client’s enquiry to coordinated supply.

  1. 01Manufacturer search

    Identifying Chinese manufacturers and suppliers that match the product’s technical specifications, budget and order volume.

  2. 02Quote and price comparison

    Comparing offers on price, MOQ, production lead time, packaging and delivery terms.

  3. 03Order coordination

    Tracking specifications, order details, production lead times and communication with the supplier.

  4. 04Quality inspection

    Arranging inspection of products and packaging against the agreed specification.

  5. 05Logistics and export documents

    Coordinating shipping options, cargo details and export documentation with partners.

04 How we work

Clear stages, confirmed specifications and transparent communication.

  1. Stage 1

    Enquiry

    Product, model, OEM code, technical specifications, quantity and delivery location are clarified.

  2. Stage 2

    Product identification

    Manufacturer and supplier options that meet the requirements are researched.

  3. Stage 3

    Quotations

    Price, MOQ, production lead time and delivery terms are collected.

  4. Stage 4

    Order confirmation

    Specification, price and order terms are agreed with the client.

  5. Stage 5

    Production and preparation

    Production and shipment readiness are coordinated with the supplier.

  6. Stage 6

    Export and shipping

    Documentation and international transport are coordinated with partners.
